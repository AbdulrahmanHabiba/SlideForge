// GOOD TO KNOW

Clear answers.
Zero fine print.

Everything you should know before you convert.

Is SlideForge really free?

Yes. The application itself has no subscriptions, ads, or tracking. Hosting a public server may have infrastructure costs for the site owner.

Can I download any Scribd document?

No. Scribd controls download permissions and may require an account or subscription. Download files through Scribd when authorized, then upload your file here for conversion.

Are PowerPoint exports fully editable?

A PPTX rebuilt from images preserves the appearance of each slide, but text and diagrams become images rather than editable shapes. Original uploaded PPTX files remain editable.

What's the difference between HD and Standard?

HD uses higher resolution and quality for rasterized slides. Standard creates smaller image-based exports. Original PDF and PPTX files are preserved when you download in their original format.

Where are my documents stored?

There are no accounts or saved document libraries. Files are processed by the running server and returned immediately; the browser keeps the ZIP in memory until you leave or refresh the page.
